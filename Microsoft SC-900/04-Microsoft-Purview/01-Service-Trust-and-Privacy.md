# Module Overview

Microsoft's cloud services operate on a foundation of security, privacy, compliance, and trust.

Organizations need assurance that Microsoft protects the information they place in Azure, Microsoft 365, and other cloud services.

They also need documentation showing how Microsoft implements relevant security and compliance requirements.

This module introduces two fundamental concepts:

**Microsoft Service Trust Portal (STP)**

**Microsoft's Privacy Principles**

## The Big Idea

Organizations need answers to two questions:

**How can we verify Microsoft's security and compliance practices?**

The **Service Trust Portal** provides supporting documentation.

**What commitments does Microsoft make about protecting customer information?**

Microsoft's **privacy principles** explain its approach to customer data and privacy.

### ELI5

Imagine an organization hires a company to store its sensitive records.

Before signing the contract, the organization wants to know:

- How does the company protect the records?
- Has anyone independently evaluated its security?
- Where will the records be stored?
- Who controls the information?
- What happens if a government agency requests access?
- How can the organization verify compliance claims?

The Service Trust Portal provides relevant documentation.

Microsoft's privacy commitments explain how Microsoft approaches those responsibilities.

### Master Memory Trick

**Service Trust Portal = SHOW ME THE EVIDENCE**

**Privacy Principles = HOW MICROSOFT HANDLES MY DATA**

# 3. Independent Audit Reports

## Big Idea

An independent audit report documents an evaluation performed by an external auditor.

Examples of recognized assurance or certification frameworks include:

- SOC reports
- ISO certifications
- Other applicable standards and regulatory assessments

## Why Independent Audits Matter

A cloud provider can describe its own security practices.

Independent audit reports provide additional assurance by documenting an external evaluation of applicable controls.

### ELI5

An organization says:

> We follow strong security procedures.

An independent auditor examines relevant controls and issues a report.

That report helps customers evaluate the claim.

### Important Limitation

An audit report applies to a defined scope.

It may cover particular:

- Services
- Controls
- Locations
- Audit periods

An audit report for one service does not automatically demonstrate that every Microsoft service or customer workload meets the same requirements.

# 21. Privacy Commitment 1 — Data Control

## Big Idea

Customers retain control over the information they place in Microsoft cloud services.

Microsoft describes customer rights to access, modify, and delete customer data, subject to the applicable service and contractual arrangements.

Microsoft also states that it uses customer information in accordance with agreed service purposes.

### ELI5

Imagine placing important organizational records in secure off-site storage.

The storage company provides the facility and protection.

However, it does not automatically become the owner of the records.

The organization retains responsibility for deciding how its information should be used.

### Important Concepts

**Customer data remains under customer control.**

**Microsoft is not automatically authorized to use customer content for unrelated purposes.**

# 27. Data Residency vs. Data Sovereignty

This is supplemental GRC context that helps explain the distinction.

## Data Residency

Concerns the physical or geographic location of data.

## Data Sovereignty

Concerns the legal jurisdiction and rules that govern data.

### Example

An organization stores customer information in a particular country.

**Residency:** The data is stored there.

**Sovereignty:** Applicable laws and jurisdiction determine legal obligations involving that data.

### Important Concept

**Data location and legal jurisdiction are related but not interchangeable.**

# 34. Government and Law Enforcement Requests

Microsoft's stated approach includes:

**Scrutinize the request.**

Determine whether the demand is legally valid and appropriate.

**Notify the customer where legally permitted.**

Microsoft may provide information about the request when the law allows.

**Direct requests to the customer when appropriate.**

Microsoft's stated position is that government agencies should generally seek customer information directly from the customer.

**Challenge inappropriate demands where legally possible.**

Microsoft describes challenging government requests inconsistent with applicable legal protections.

### Important Limitation

Microsoft may be required to disclose information under applicable law.

Its privacy commitments do not mean Microsoft can disregard all legally binding demands.

### Memory Trick

**DATA DEFENSE = Legal scrutiny + notification + challenge where permitted**

# 36. Privacy vs. Security vs. Compliance

These terms are related but not interchangeable.

## Privacy

Concerned with appropriate collection, use, control, and protection of personal information.

## Security

Concerned with protecting information and systems against unauthorized access, modification, and disruption.

## Compliance

Concerned with satisfying applicable requirements, including laws, regulations, standards, and organizational obligations.

| Concept | Main question |
|---|---|
| Privacy | Is information being handled appropriately? |
| Security | Is information being protected? |
| Compliance | Are applicable requirements being met? |

### Example

An organization encrypts its database.

That supports security.

However, if the organization collects personal information without an appropriate lawful basis or uses it for an unauthorized purpose, privacy concerns may remain.

### Key Concept

**Security supports privacy, but security alone does not guarantee privacy or compliance.**

# 41. Audit Evidence and Control Inheritance

## Big Idea

Cloud customers may rely on certain security controls implemented by a cloud service provider.

This is sometimes called:

**Control Inheritance**

### ELI5

A customer using a cloud service may rely on Microsoft for applicable underlying physical security controls.

The customer does not personally operate Microsoft's datacenters.

However, the customer still needs sufficient assurance that those inherited protections meet applicable requirements.

### Example

A cloud security assessment includes physical datacenter protection requirements.

Microsoft's applicable audit documentation may help demonstrate the provider's implementation of those controls.

### Important Limitation

The customer must still verify that:

- The assessment applies to the relevant service.
- The relevant environment is in scope.
- The evidence covers the required control.
- Customer-specific controls remain appropriately implemented.

**Provider audit evidence does not automatically replace the customer's own control assessment.**

# 43. Evaluating Cloud Provider Evidence

When reviewing evidence, an assessor should consider:

**Scope**

Does the documentation apply to the relevant cloud service?

**Date**

Is the report current enough for the assessment?

**Control Coverage**

Does it address the specific requirement?

**Responsibility**

Is the control implemented by Microsoft, the customer, or both?

**Exceptions**

Does the audit report identify findings or limitations?

**Documentation**

Can the organization demonstrate how it evaluated and relied on the evidence?

### Practical GRC Principle

**An audit document is evidence to evaluate, not a substitute for evaluating the control.**

# 45. Final Mental Model

Imagine an organization is evaluating Microsoft as a cloud provider.

The organization needs assurance about Microsoft's security practices.

It opens the **Service Trust Portal**.

The security team locates relevant certifications, audit reports, and supporting documentation.

The compliance team reviews materials associated with applicable industries, regions, and regulatory requirements.

Important documents are saved to **My Library**.

Notifications are configured so the team can identify updated evidence.

The organization also evaluates Microsoft's privacy commitments.

**Data Control** explains how the customer retains control of its information.

**Data Location** explains Microsoft's supported geographic storage choices.

**Data Security** explains Microsoft's commitments concerning encryption and protection.

**Data Defense** explains how Microsoft addresses government and law enforcement requests for customer information.

The organization then evaluates the provider's evidence alongside its own responsibilities.

This supports a broader objective:

**Establish trust through documented controls, independent assurance, privacy commitments, and shared accountability.**

That is the central concept of this module.
