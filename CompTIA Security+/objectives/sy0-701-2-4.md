# SY0-701 objective 2.4: Indicators and malicious activity

[Security+ index](../README.md) · Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Malware classes describe behavior: ransomware encrypts or blocks access for extortion; a worm self-propagates; a trojan masquerades as benign; spyware gathers information; a rootkit hides privileged persistence. Attacks may also involve DDoS, DNS manipulation, on-path interception, wireless abuse, password spraying, brute force, replay and application injection. Password spraying tries a few common passwords against many accounts, often to avoid lockout; brute force exhaustively guesses one target.

Indicators include unusual account lockouts, impossible or concurrent sessions, unexpected outbound traffic, blocked content, altered files and abnormal resource use. An indicator is a lead, not proof. Correlate timestamps, identity, endpoint, network and application logs; preserve evidence and avoid premature attribution.

## Apply it

Several accounts each show one failed login from the same source followed by one success. Investigate password spraying and the successful session, reset affected credentials, and check MFA and source context.

## Active recall

How would you distinguish a worm from a trojan, spraying from brute force, and a suspicious signal from a confirmed incident?
