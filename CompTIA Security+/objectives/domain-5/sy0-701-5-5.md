# SY0-701 objective 5.5: Audits and assessments

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

An assessment tests control design or operating effectiveness against criteria; an audit provides structured assurance with defined scope, independence and evidence. Internal and external reviews serve different audiences and levels of independence. Plan scope, criteria, sampling and access to evidence; interview owners, inspect artifacts and test actual operation. A penetration test probes exploitable paths under agreed rules of engagement and does not replace broad control assessment.

Findings should identify condition, criterion, cause or context, impact, owner and corrective action. Management responds and tracks remediation; independent validation confirms closure. Be clear about sampling limits and dates so a favorable result is not overstated.

## Deeper distinctions

The scope, criteria, independence and method distinguish review activities. A vulnerability scan identifies potential weaknesses; a penetration test probes exploit chains under rules of engagement; a control assessment checks design and/or operation; an audit offers structured assurance against specified criteria. Internal and external reviewers can both add value, but their independence and reporting obligations differ.

Plan sampling, time period and access before fieldwork. Collect original or traceable evidence, interview owners, observe processes and reproduce technical tests where appropriate. A finding should connect criterion, observed condition, affected scope, risk and corrective action. Management owns response and remediation, while closure requires evidence-based retest. A clean sample is not a universal guarantee; state limitations explicitly.

**Assessment drill:** An SSP claims all privileged accounts have MFA. Compare the account inventory, identity policy, exception list and actual sign-in evidence. What finding would you write if service accounts are omitted?

## Additional blueprint concepts

Attestation is a formal assertion about a control or state; an independent audit may examine evidence behind it. Internal self-assessment and audit-committee review differ from external regulatory examination or independent third-party assessment in audience and independence.

Penetration tests can be physical or cyber and may emphasize offensive discovery, defensive response or integrated red/blue activity. A known-environment test provides detailed system knowledge, a partially known test some context, and an unknown-environment test little prior information. Passive reconnaissance observes public or existing information; active reconnaissance probes a target and may trigger monitoring. Every test needs scope, authorization and rules of engagement.

## Apply it

A reviewer samples privileged accounts and finds two without approval. Preserve the sample and criteria, confirm facts with the owner, assess exposure, open remediation and retest after correction.
