# SY0-701 objective 5.2: Risk management

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact, then assess and prioritize risk against organizational tolerance. A risk register records scenarios, owners, ratings, treatment, due dates and status. Responses include mitigate, avoid, transfer or accept; transfer (such as insurance) does not remove operational responsibility. A business impact analysis identifies critical processes, dependencies and effects of disruption; key risk indicators signal changing exposure.

Quantitative methods use values such as single-loss expectancy (SLE = asset value × exposure factor) and annualized-loss expectancy (ALE = SLE × annualized rate of occurrence), where defensible estimates exist. Qualitative ratings use defined likelihood/impact scales. Report assumptions and residual risk; the appropriate authority, not the analyst alone, accepts material risk.

## Deeper distinctions

Risk is a scenario with uncertain consequences, not just a CVE score. Describe a threat acting on a vulnerability in an asset and its plausible impact. Inherent risk precedes controls; residual risk remains after treatment. Use a risk register with owner, likelihood, impact, assumptions, treatment, due date and review trigger. Risk appetite or tolerance guides escalation, but a control team should not silently accept risk for the business.

Qualitative analysis uses defined categories; quantitative analysis uses estimates with uncertainty. SLE = asset value × exposure factor, and ALE = SLE × annualized rate of occurrence. These are decision aids, not precise predictions. A business impact analysis identifies critical processes, dependencies, maximum tolerable disruption and recovery priorities; it informs RTO/RPO and continuity planning. Key risk indicators should signal meaningful change rather than merely count activities.

**Risk drill:** For a vulnerable public service, compare patching, segmentation, temporary shutdown, insurance and formal acceptance. State which options reduce likelihood, impact, both or neither.

## Additional blueprint concepts

Assessments may be one-time, recurring, continuous or ad hoc, depending on trigger and decision need. A risk threshold is a specific point for escalation; appetite is the broad amount/type of risk an organization is willing to pursue, and tolerance is the acceptable variation around objectives. Exception and exemption are governed departures from requirements with different local meanings—check the organization's definitions rather than assuming universal labels.

In a BIA, RTO is the targeted time to restore a service and RPO the targeted maximum data loss measured in time. MTTR estimates average repair duration after failures; MTBF estimates average operating time between failures for repairable assets. These are different metrics and do not by themselves set acceptable business downtime. ARO means expected frequency per year; ALE = SLE × ARO. Treat estimates and uncertainty honestly.

## Apply it

A new public-facing service has an unpatched flaw. Record the scenario and exposure, compare remediation options and deadlines, assign an owner, and escalate any residual risk to the authorized decision-maker.

## Active recall

Explain risk versus vulnerability and inherent versus residual risk. Compute ALE from an SLE of $20,000 and annualized rate of 0.25.
