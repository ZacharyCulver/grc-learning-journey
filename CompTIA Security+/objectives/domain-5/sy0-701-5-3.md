# SY0-701 objective 5.3: Third-party risk

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

A vendor can introduce access, data handling and supply-chain dependencies. Before selection, perform due diligence suited to the service: questionnaires, independent assessment reports, audit evidence, testing rights, subcontractor and data-flow review. During the relationship, monitor changes, incidents, performance and expiration of assurance; at exit, revoke access and verify data return or destruction.

Agreement terms serve distinct purposes: an SLA sets measurable service commitments, an NDA governs confidentiality, an MSA sets overarching commercial terms, and a statement of work defines specific deliverables. MOUs or MOAs document collaboration, while right-to-audit and rules of engagement define assurance or testing rights. A questionnaire is evidence to evaluate, not proof of actual security.

## Apply it

A cloud vendor hosts sensitive data. Verify location, encryption and key responsibilities, incident-notification terms, independent reports, subcontractors, recovery objectives and offboarding evidence before approval.

## Active recall

What would you seek beyond a vendor's self-attestation? Distinguish an SLA from a right-to-audit clause.
