# SY0-701 objective 5.3: Third-party risk

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

A vendor can introduce access, data handling and supply-chain dependencies. Before selection, perform due diligence suited to the service: questionnaires, independent assessment reports, audit evidence, testing rights, subcontractor and data-flow review. During the relationship, monitor changes, incidents, performance and expiration of assurance; at exit, revoke access and verify data return or destruction.

Agreement terms serve distinct purposes: an SLA sets measurable service commitments, an NDA governs confidentiality, an MSA sets overarching commercial terms, and a statement of work defines specific deliverables. MOUs or MOAs document collaboration, while right-to-audit and rules of engagement define assurance or testing rights. A questionnaire is evidence to evaluate, not proof of actual security.

## Deeper distinctions

Third-party risk starts before contract award and continues through renewal and exit. Classify the service by data, connectivity, criticality and substitutability. Due diligence may include scope-matched independent reports, questionnaires, penetration-test summaries, subcontractor inventory, incident history and recovery testing. An audit report on one service or date does not prove every product is secure; review exceptions and customer responsibilities.

Contract terms should define security responsibilities, data location and return/deletion, incident notice, audit rights, testing rules, service levels and termination. SLA concerns measurable service performance; NDA confidentiality; MSA general terms; SOW specific deliverables. A right-to-audit clause grants the ability to obtain assurance, not an audit result. Monitor performance and material changes, then revoke access and validate data disposition at exit.

**Vendor drill:** A SaaS provider asks for privileged API access. Specify minimum scope, expiry, logging, incident notice, reassessment trigger and offboarding evidence.

## Additional blueprint concepts

Vendor selection includes conflicts of interest as well as security due diligence. A business partners agreement may address how partners work together; a work order or SOW names the particular work and deliverables. These are not substitutes for an SLA, NDA or right-to-audit term. Rules of engagement constrain any penetration test against a vendor environment. Review independent reports and supply-chain dependencies, then monitor the vendor after contract award rather than treating one questionnaire as permanent approval.

## Apply it

A cloud vendor hosts sensitive data. Verify location, encryption and key responsibilities, incident-notification terms, independent reports, subcontractors, recovery objectives and offboarding evidence before approval.

## Active recall

What would you seek beyond a vendor's self-attestation? Distinguish an SLA from a right-to-audit clause.
