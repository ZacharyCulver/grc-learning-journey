# SY0-701 objective 2.1: Threat actors and motivations

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Distinguish an actor's access, resources and objective. Nation-state operations may be patient, well funded and espionage-driven; organized crime often pursues money; hacktivists seek attention or political effect; insiders may have legitimate access; unskilled attackers can still exploit widely available tools. Shadow IT is unsanctioned technology and creates unmanaged exposure, not necessarily a malicious actor. Internal versus external access and sophistication are separate dimensions.

Motives include data theft, service disruption, extortion, financial gain, ideological impact and espionage. Do not infer an actor's identity solely from one indicator; the same phishing lure or malware can be reused by different groups. Prioritize defense around capability, access and likely impact rather than labels.

## Deeper distinctions

An **actor** is who may conduct the activity; a **vector** is how the environment is entered; a **motive** is why. Do not collapse these into one label. A contractor with authorized credentials can be an insider even if physically remote; an external actor can acquire the same credentials. Shadow IT denotes unmanaged systems or services, which expand attack surface without proving malicious intent.

Capability, opportunity and intent help prioritize a scenario. A well-resourced state actor can pursue long-term access and espionage, but a commodity criminal campaign can still cause catastrophic ransomware loss. Indicators rarely establish attribution: IP addresses, tools and language can be rented, shared or planted. Report observed behavior and confidence separately from hypotheses.

**Decision drill:** If a phishing payload requests payroll changes, the immediate control is independent payment verification and account containment. The actor's presumed nationality does not change the first response.

## Apply it

A departing employee with valid cloud access downloads large data sets. Treat as a potentially harmful insider event and preserve evidence without claiming motive before investigation.

## Active recall

For nation-state, criminal group, hacktivist and careless insider, give a plausible motive and one defensive priority.
