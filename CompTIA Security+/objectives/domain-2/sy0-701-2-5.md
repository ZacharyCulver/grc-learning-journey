# SY0-701 objective 2.5: Enterprise mitigation techniques

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Segmentation limits lateral movement; access controls and permissions constrain who can reach what; encryption protects information in supported states. Hardening removes unnecessary services, ports, protocols and software, replaces default passwords, deploys host firewalls or endpoint protection, and uses application allowlisting where appropriate. Decommissioning securely removes obsolete assets and their accounts, routes and data. Choose a mitigation that interrupts the specific attack path.

Defense in depth combines different safeguards rather than duplicating one weak layer. Validate that a configuration is deployed and effective, monitor drift, patch when feasible, and document approved exceptions with owners and expiration. An unpatchable device may require isolation and compensating controls, but that does not erase its residual risk.

## Deeper distinctions

Choose mitigation by attack path. Patching removes a known weakness, segmentation restricts reach, least privilege constrains actions, and allowlisting blocks unapproved execution. Hardening includes closing unused ports, removing services, replacing defaults, secure configuration and endpoint protection. Encryption may reduce data disclosure but does not fix a vulnerable web application or stop service disruption.

Controls should be verified in practice. A VLAN by itself is not proof that traffic cannot cross segments; inspect routing/firewall policy and test. An application allowlist without update governance can block legitimate patches or be bypassed by trusted interpreters. An unpatchable system needs isolation, monitoring, documented ownership and a retirement path. Exceptions should state the reason, risk, compensating measures, expiration and approval.

**Decision drill:** A legacy server has an exposed management interface. Compare “install EDR,” “restrict management to a jump host,” and “encrypt the disk.” Which directly removes the remote path, which improves detection, and which mainly reduces loss after physical theft?

## Apply it

An unsupported controller must remain online. Limit its network segment and administrative paths, monitor flows, maintain an owner and replacement plan, and obtain explicit risk acceptance.

## Active recall

Name one preventive and one detective measure for an exposed service; explain what you would verify after applying them.
