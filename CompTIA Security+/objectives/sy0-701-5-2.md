# SY0-701 objective 5.2: Risk management

[Security+ index](../README.md) · Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact, then assess and prioritize risk against organizational tolerance. A risk register records scenarios, owners, ratings, treatment, due dates and status. Responses include mitigate, avoid, transfer or accept; transfer (such as insurance) does not remove operational responsibility. A business impact analysis identifies critical processes, dependencies and effects of disruption; key risk indicators signal changing exposure.

Quantitative methods use values such as single-loss expectancy (SLE = asset value × exposure factor) and annualized-loss expectancy (ALE = SLE × annualized rate of occurrence), where defensible estimates exist. Qualitative ratings use defined likelihood/impact scales. Report assumptions and residual risk; the appropriate authority, not the analyst alone, accepts material risk.

## Apply it

A new public-facing service has an unpatched flaw. Record the scenario and exposure, compare remediation options and deadlines, assign an owner, and escalate any residual risk to the authorized decision-maker.

## Active recall

Explain risk versus vulnerability and inherent versus residual risk. Compute ALE from an SLE of $20,000 and annualized rate of 0.25.
