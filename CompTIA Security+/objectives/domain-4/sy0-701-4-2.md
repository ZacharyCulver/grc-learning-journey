# SY0-701 objective 4.2: Asset management

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 4: Security Operations

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

You cannot secure or retire an asset you do not know exists. Track procurement, assignment, owner, location, classification, software and data throughout its lifecycle. Reconcile inventory with network discovery and cloud accounts; unexpected assets and stale accounts are investigation leads. Owners make risk and retention decisions, while custodians operate safeguards.

At disposal, meet retention and legal-hold requirements, remove accounts and network access, sanitize storage with an appropriate method, and document destruction or transfer. Deleting a file is not necessarily sanitization; verify the procedure and retain chain-of-custody or certificate evidence where required.

## Deeper distinctions

Asset management starts before deployment. Record purchase/procurement approval, owner, purpose, serial or cloud identifier, software, data classification and location. Network enumeration can reveal unmanaged assets, but disconnected or ephemeral assets also require procurement/cloud reconciliation. An inventory is useful only if ownership and status are current.

At transfer or retirement, identify retention and legal-hold duties before destruction. Remove access, credentials, certificates and routes; sanitize media at an assurance level suited to the data and device; retain evidence of disposal. Encryption may simplify certain disposal decisions only if keys can be securely destroyed and policy allows it. A certificate of destruction is evidence of a process, not proof that the original inventory was complete.

**Lifecycle drill:** For a contractor laptop and a cloud test database, document acquisition, classification, assignment, periodic reconciliation and offboarding separately.

## Additional blueprint concepts

The official lifecycle explicitly includes **certification of disposal**: retain documentation that sanitization or destruction occurred and reconcile the certificate to the actual asset. Retention requirements may prevent immediate destruction, while legal hold can override routine disposal. Enumerating hosts can find deployed assets, but it misses offline equipment and some short-lived cloud resources, so reconcile with procurement, assignment and cloud inventories.

## Apply it

A laptop assigned to a departing contractor is missing. Use inventory to identify its data and owner, disable associated access, assess encryption and exposure, and record recovery or disposal evidence.

## Active recall

Trace one server from procurement to decommissioning, naming an owner, classification, inventory update and final sanitization record.
