# SY0-701 objective 4.8: Incident response

[Security+ index](../../README.md) · Domain 4: Security Operations

Original study notes aligned to the public CompTIA SY0-701 V7 blueprint; not official training or exam questions.

## Explain it

Prepare roles, communications, logging, tools and exercises before an event. During a suspected incident, detect, analyze, contain, eradicate and recover, then capture lessons learned; real cases may loop between phases. Containment limits harm while preserving essential evidence. Root-cause analysis addresses how the incident occurred, not just the visible symptom.

Forensics needs documented acquisition, preservation, time and source metadata, chain of custody and any legal hold. Tabletop exercises test decisions; simulations test actions. Coordinate reporting with legal, leadership and affected stakeholders under applicable policy. Restoration is complete only after verifying normal operation and watching for recurrence.

## Deeper distinctions

Preparation defines roles, escalation, communications, evidence handling and exercise cadence. Detection identifies a signal; analysis tests whether it is an incident and scopes affected identities and assets. Containment can be immediate (isolate host) or strategic (segment a network), balancing business continuity and evidence preservation. Eradication removes root cause and persistence; recovery restores verified clean operation while monitoring for recurrence. Lessons learned update controls and playbooks.

Digital evidence needs acquisition method, timestamps, integrity checks, custody history and legal-hold decisions. Volatile memory may disappear on shutdown; a hasty wipe can destroy the timeline. Notify legal and leadership according to applicable obligations without promising an unverified scope. Threat hunting proactively tests hypotheses; root-cause analysis examines why controls failed, not just which file was malicious.

**Response drill:** A server has an active suspicious session. State what to log or capture before disconnecting it, when immediate isolation outweighs collection, and who authorizes business-impacting containment.

## Additional blueprint concepts

Legal hold preserves relevant data when required by litigation or investigation; it may suspend normal deletion. E-discovery identifies, preserves and produces electronically stored information under a legal process. Chain of custody records who had evidence, when, why and how it was transferred. Acquisition should be repeatable and integrity-checked; report conclusions with scope and uncertainty. In an incident, coordinate these duties with counsel before routine cleanup destroys relevant material.

## Apply it

A ransomware alert appears on one workstation. Isolate it under the playbook, preserve volatile and durable evidence as appropriate, investigate spread, coordinate communications, eradicate persistence and restore from tested clean backups.

## Active recall

Explain why immediately wiping every suspicious host can damage an investigation. What proves recovery was successful?
