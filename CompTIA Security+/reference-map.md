## How the other attached references fit

Jason Dion's *Security+ (SY0-701) Study Notes* (405 PDF pages) is a fuller explanation to consult when the short objective-aligned treatments are insufficient, especially for networking, monitoring and incident response. Ken Underhill's *CompTIA Security+ (701) Study Notes* (80 PDF pages) groups related concepts across objectives: fundamentals and crypto (printed pp. 4–16), IAM and architecture (17–29), resilience and vulnerabilities (30–42), endpoint/network/application defenses (43–53), investigations and malicious activity (54–66), and governance/risk/compliance (67–79). Their organization is not a one-to-one match to the exam objectives.

Artem Polynko's *Full Learning Guide* (53 PDF pages) and *Quick Exam Refresher* (7 pages) can support rapid review. The separate *100 Questions & Answers* (52 pages) is private practice: work questions in the original PDF, then write only your own missed-concept summaries here. Its domain-assignment table has an apparent duplicate Q35, and the materials describe a 750/900 scaled passing score as “about 83%,” then infer a number of questions one can miss. **Do not interpret a scaled passing score as a raw percent-correct threshold or use that table to calculate readiness.** Track your practice accuracy by objective and read the answer explanations critically.

## Suggested workflow

Read the objective in CompTIA's blueprint; study the matching Ramdayal and Messer pages; use Dion or Underhill when you cannot explain a mechanism; complete original practice in the source material; write a one-paragraph error analysis in your own words; and retest the concept later. Record the *why* behind the answer rather than memorizing wording. Treat any source conflict as a prompt to verify against the official objectives and current primary technical documentation.
